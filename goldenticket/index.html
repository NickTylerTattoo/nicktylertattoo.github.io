Questions

Good to know

What's No-Lose?

Add $50 to any pack and you also get a $100 gift card. Win or not, $100 comes back to you. The Max with No-Lose is $230 for all 20 entries plus the gift card.

How do the gift cards work?

Gift cards, from prizes or No-Lose, come off the balance of a Half or Full session on the day of your tattoo. You still pay the usual deposit when you book. They're good for at least 9 years.

How many entries can I get?

Up to 20 per person, from packs and free entries combined. Grab a pack, enter free once a day, or both, until you hit 20.

Is there a free way to enter?

Yes. Claim a free entry once a day, no purchase needed. Free entries count exactly the same as pack entries, and everyone has the same 20-entry max.

Who can enter?

Anyone 18 or older who is a legal resident of New York, New Jersey or Connecticut. New York law doesn't allow tattooing anyone under 18.

When and where is the drawing?

Sunday, November 22 at 8 PM, live on Instagram @nicktylertattoo. Entries close at 6 PM that day. Turn on notifications so you don't miss it.

How will I know if I won?

I'll text every winner within 2 hours of the live with a private booking link. Book your date within 14 days.

What kind of tattoo can I get?

I work in black and grey only: fine line, ornamental, floral, geometric and blackwork. If you're dreaming in color, I'm happy to point you to someone great.

Where is the studio?

A private studio in Selden, Long Island. Winners get the exact address when they book.

Do prizes expire or transfer?

Prize sessions are booked within 14 days of the draw, for a date from December 9, 2026 through February 27, 2027. They're for the winner and can't be traded for cash. Gift cards are good for at least 9 years and can be given to someone else.

Official rules

  • Prizes: one Full session ($1,500 value), one Half session ($750 value) and five $250 gift cards, $3,500 total. If 400 total entries are received, five $100 gift cards are added. If 600 are received, one more Half session ($750 value) is added. Maximum total prize value: $4,750.

  • Entries past the 20 max don't count, and any purchase past it is refunded.
