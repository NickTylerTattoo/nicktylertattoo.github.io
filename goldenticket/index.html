Questions

Good to know

What's No-Lose?

Add $50 to any pack and you also get a $100 gift card. Win or not, $100 comes back to you. The Max with No-Lose is $200 for all 20 entries plus the gift card.

How do the gift cards work?

Gift cards, from prizes or No-Lose, come off the balance of a Half or Full session on the day of your tattoo. You still pay the usual deposit when you book.

How many entries can I get?

Everyone gets one free entry, plus up to 20 more from packs. That's 21 max per person.

Is there a free way to enter?

Yes. Everyone can claim one free entry, no purchase needed. It goes in the same draw as pack entries.

Who can enter?

Anyone 18 or older who is a legal resident of New York, New Jersey or Connecticut. New York law doesn't allow tattooing anyone under 18.

When and where is the drawing?

Sunday, November 22 at 8 PM, live on Instagram @nicktylertattoo. Entries close at 6 PM that day. Turn on notifications so you don't miss it.

How will I know if I won?

I'll text every winner within 2 hours of the live with a private booking link. Book your date within 14 days.

What kind of tattoo can I get?

I work in black and grey only: fine line, ornamental, floral, geometric and blackwork. If you're dreaming in color, I'm happy to point you to someone great.

Where is the studio?

A private studio in Selden, Long Island. Winners get the exact address when they book.

Do prizes expire or transfer?

Prize sessions are booked within 14 days of the draw, for a date from December 9, 2026 through February 27, 2027. They're for the winner and can't be traded for cash. Gift cards can be given to someone else.

Official rules
  • No purchase necessary to enter or win. Everyone can claim one free entry. Packs add up to 20 more entries per person. Every entry has the same chance of being drawn, so more entries mean better odds.
  • Entry period: Sunday, Nov 1, 2026 at 6:00 PM ET to Sunday, Nov 22, 2026 at 6:00 PM ET.
  • Free entry: 1 per person for the whole giveaway. Pack entries: up to 20 per person. Opting in to texts or emails is optional and never affects your entries or odds.
  • Drawing: Sunday, Nov 22, 2026 at 8:00 PM ET, live on Instagram. Odds depend on the number of entries received.
  • Prizes: one Full session ($1,500 value), one Half session ($750 value) and five $250 gift cards, $3,500 total. For every 50 total entries received, free and paid, one more winner is added with a $100 gift card, up to 15 added winners. Maximum: 22 winners and $5,000 in total prize value.
  • Winners are texted within 2 hours of the drawing and book within 14 days. Prize sessions run December 9, 2026 through February 27, 2027.
  • No-Lose adds a $100 gift card to any pack for $50. All gift cards apply to the balance of a Half or Full session.
  • Pack entries past 20 per person don't count, and any purchase past it is refunded.
  • Sponsor: Nick Tyler Tattoo, Selden, NY. This giveaway isn't sponsored, endorsed or run by Instagram.
