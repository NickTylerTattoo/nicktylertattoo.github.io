Have it built for you

Everything in this guide runs in my studio from one place. I call it the Private Studio System, and I’m opening a done-for-you version to a small group of artists first.

  • Built for you. Your request form, booking calendar, deposits and every text, set up for your studio and written in your voice.
  • Built by a tattooer who runs it. It’s the system in my own studio, not a template, made for custom work: approve or decline, the right session length, a deposit to book.
  • Made for solo artists and private studios. One chair, by appointment.
  • Software included. One account with everything in this guide under one roof, so there are no separate form, texting, email or automation apps to connect.
  • One monthly payment.
  • Ad management, if you want it. I can run the Meta ads that send people to your request form, the same way I run mine: a submitted request is the only result that counts.

You’d still do your two steps: approve the request, and do the tattoo. I’d do the build.

Prices are set at launch, and the list hears them first. You’re already on it because you signed up for this guide.

Hold my founding spot

Leave your name and email and I’ll hold one for you. There’s nothing to pay today.

And if you’d rather build it yourself, everything you need is in lessons 1 to 9. Either way, I hope it gets your next request booked.
