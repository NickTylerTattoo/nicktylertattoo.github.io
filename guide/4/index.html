Approve and book

Nothing goes on my calendar until the deposit is paid, and the client books themselves, so I never chase a deposit.

How I run it. I read each request and add one tag, Half or Full. That tag texts the client my booking link in my words, set to the right session length. They pick an open date and pay the deposit to hold it. My menu has two sessions: a Half, up to 3 hours with a $150 deposit, and a Full, up to 6 hours with a $250 deposit.

What you’d need: a booking app that takes the deposit at booking, and a card processor behind it. With separate apps, “one tag sends the link” becomes a saved reply you paste, picking the right link each time.
