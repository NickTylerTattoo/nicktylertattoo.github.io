After the session

The texts that bring clients back go out on their own, long after I’ve moved on to the next tattoo.

How I run it. At checkout the deposit comes off the total, and I log the balance on the client’s record, cash or card. After the session, the aftercare text goes out with my Google review link. Approved clients who go quiet get a check-in on day 2 and a last one around day 7, and the request closes after 30 days. Every client gets a birthday text from me each year, with an offer to come back.

What you’d need: a way to take the balance, the texting service and automation app from lesson 3 for aftercare and check-ins, your review link, and an email tool for birthdays and anything you send your whole client list. Card fees for tapping a card in person are in lesson 4.

Your Google review link is free from your Business Profile, and Google prohibits rewarding clients for reviews. If you’d rather pay for review requests, NiceJob sends them by text and email from $75 a month.
