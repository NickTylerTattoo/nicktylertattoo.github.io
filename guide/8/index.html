The do-it-yourself bill

A setup where the texts go out on their own runs about $180 to $190 a month in software (about $150 to $160 billed yearly), plus card fees and 15 to 25 hours to build. The cheapest setup costs $0 a month, and you type the texts yourself.

The hours are my estimates for a solo artist with 30 to 40 bookings a month. Every one of these apps works. The real cost is connecting them and keeping them connected: when one connection breaks, a text quietly stops going out.
