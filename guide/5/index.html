Before the session

The client knows when, where and how to prepare, and I don’t type any of it.

How I run it. The moment the deposit is paid, the client gets a confirmation text with my prep page. The day before, a reminder goes out with the time, the prep page and my consent form. When they sign it, I get a notice, so I know who’s ready before they walk in.

What it saves you. Two texts per booking, the paper consent form, and every “where are you located?” message.

What you’d need: a confirmation, a day-before reminder, a prep page, and a consent form clients can sign on their phone. Most booking apps in lesson 4 send the confirmation and reminders for you. Text reminders are free on Square Appointments and come with Calendly Standard and Setmore Pro; Acuity and Bookedin charge more for them. A prep page can be a page on your website or a shared doc, for free.
