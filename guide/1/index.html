Why I wrote this

My studio answers every tattoo request while I’m tattooing, and nothing goes on my calendar without a deposit. This guide shows you how that works, every app you could build it with yourself, and what it costs in money and hours.

I’m not teaching anyone to tattoo. You already can. This is the business side, the part nobody taught either of us.

Most artists lose bookings in the same three places:

  • A request sits in your DMs until the client books someone else.
  • A date goes on the calendar with no deposit, and the chair sits empty.
  • You type the same confirmations, reminders and aftercare texts all day.

From my own records: 1,500+ tattoo requests through one form, 1,100+ bookings with a deposit on every one, 450+ birthday texts sent for me, and 131 Google reviews.

Here’s what’s inside:

  1. The framework: one request, nine steps, two of them yours.
  2. Every feature: how I run it, then the free and paid apps that can do it, with prices checked in October 2026.
  3. The do-it-yourself bill: software, texting and card fees, and setup hours.
  4. My real client texts and deposit policy, ready to copy.
