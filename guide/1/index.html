Why I built it

My studio answers every tattoo request while I’m tattooing, and nothing goes on my calendar without a deposit. I don’t type the confirmations, the reminders, the aftercare or the follow-ups. This guide shows you how it works, what it gives back, and what it would take to build it yourself.

The second job nobody warns you about

If you book your own clients, you’re running a front desk out of your phone, and it never closes.

  • The messages never stop. DMs, texts, comments, emails, missed calls. Any one of them could be a booking, so you check your phone between clients, between lines and at dinner.
  • Requests land everywhere. The idea is in a DM, the photos are in a text, the placement is in a comment. You piece it together before you can even quote it.
  • You type the same answers all day. How much, when are you free, where are you, how do I prepare, how do I take care of it.
  • You chase. Dates, deposits, and people who said yes and went quiet.
  • It costs you bookings. A request that waits a day books with the next artist. A date with no deposit turns into an empty chair.

None of that is tattooing, and none of it pays. It’s a second job on top of the first one.

Two steps instead

I built a system to do that second job. Every request comes in through one form, gets an answer in seconds and lands on one board. The client books their own date and pays the deposit to hold it. Every text after that goes out on its own, in my words, until the client comes back for the next one.

My part is two steps: I approve the request, and I do the tattoo.
