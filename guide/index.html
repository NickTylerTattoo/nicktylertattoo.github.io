Questions

Fair questions.

I’m not techy. Can I do this?

Yes. The guide shows every piece in plain words, with a short video for each part. If you’d rather not build it, the done-for-you version arrives built.

My DMs work fine. Why a form?

DMs are where requests go missing and nobody pays a deposit. The form does the asking, so you only answer real requests.

I already use a booking app.

Keep it. The guide compares ten booking apps and what each one charges. The done-for-you version is for artists who want everything in one place.

No. Every message is written in a person’s voice, yours. The assistant only suggests replies, and you’re the one who sends them.
