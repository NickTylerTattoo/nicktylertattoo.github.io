Getting the request

Every request lands in one place with everything you need to say yes, and the client hears back in minutes, even while you’re tattooing.

How I run it. One form asks for the idea, placement, size and reference photos, with a checkbox agreeing to my deposit policy. The moment it’s sent, the client gets my “request received” text, I get a notice, and the request lands on my board as New. Instagram comments asking about tattoos get the form link by DM.

What it saves you. The back-and-forth to get the details, and the requests that book with someone else while you’re busy. Every request gets an answer in seconds, even mid-session.

What you’d need: a form that takes photos, a board to track requests, a texting service plus an automation app for the instant reply, and a comment-to-DM tool. Prices below are US prices from each app’s pricing page in October 2026, billed monthly, with the yearly-billing price in parentheses.
