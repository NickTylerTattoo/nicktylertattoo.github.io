Keeping it running

Three pieces make the whole thing easy to live with: one inbox, a reply assistant, and one settings page.

How I run it. Texts, Instagram DMs, Facebook messages and emails land in one inbox. A reply assistant drafts answers to common questions in my voice, and I read and send each one myself. One settings page holds my booking link, deposits and review link, and every message pulls from it, so a new link is one change, not forty.

What it saves you. Checking four apps for messages, and editing forty messages by hand when a link changes.

What you’d need, piece by piece:

  • One inbox. Meta Business Suite puts Instagram DMs and Facebook messages in one free inbox. Texts and email stay in their own apps, so you check three places.
  • Reply drafts. An AI assistant can draft replies, but you copy and paste between apps, and it only knows your prices and openings if you tell it each time.
  • One settings page. With separate apps, your booking link, deposit amounts and review link live inside each app’s messages. Change one and you hunt it down everywhere.
