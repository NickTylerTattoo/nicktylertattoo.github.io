You do two things

One request moves through nine steps, from the form to a repeat client. Two of them are yours: you approve the request, and you do the tattoo. The system does the other seven.

Everything in the middle column happens on its own, in your words. Here’s what that looks like from start to finish.

What runs behind the scenes

The moment a request comes inThe client gets my “request received” text. The request lands on my board as New, and I get a notice.The system
When I read itI add one tag, Half or Full. If it’s not a fit, I send a short, kind no myself.Me
Seconds laterThe client gets my booking link in my words, set to the right session length.The system
Day 2 and day 7If they haven’t booked, two check-ins go out with the link. After 30 days the request closes.The system
When they pay the depositThe date is held, and they get a confirmation text with my prep page.The system
The day beforeA reminder with the time, the prep page and the consent form. I get a notice when they sign it.The system
Session dayI do the tattoo. The deposit comes off the total.Me
After the sessionAftercare goes out with my Google review link.The system
Every birthdayA birthday text from me, with an offer to come back.The system

One settings page holds my booking link, deposit amounts and review link, and every message pulls from it. In my studio account, 21 automations run all of this.

At 30 bookings a month, that’s about 15 to 25 hours a month, before you count the requests that never book. That’s two to four full sessions’ worth of time, every month.

Where the money comes from

The system doesn’t tattoo for you. It stops money from slipping away between the request and the next appointment.

  • Speed. Requests get an answer in seconds, so fewer of them book with someone else while you’re busy.
  • Deposits. Every date is paid to hold, so a no-show costs the client, not you. In the last 12 months my clients paid $32,520 in deposits before they sat down.
  • Follow-ups. Approved clients who go quiet get two check-ins on their own instead of being forgotten.
  • Repeat clients. Every client gets a birthday text from you each year, with a reason to come back. Mine has sent 450+ so far.
  • Reviews. Every client gets your review link after the session. I’m at 131 Google reviews.
  • Your hours. The 15 to 25 hours a month go back into the chair, or back to you.
