Lesson 9 of 10

My texts, word for word

These are the texts my clients get, copied from my system with my links taken out. Fill in the brackets and make them sound like you.

1. Request received. The moment the form comes in.

Hey [first name], your custom tattoo request has been received!

Your request is officially in my queue and I'll be reviewing it shortly. In the meantime, feel free to check out my reviews here 👉 [your Google reviews link]

They'll give you a feel for the kind of experience I aim to provide my clients. I look forward to checking out your idea!

If I don't need any additional information from you upon review, you'll receive a booking link shortly to move forward.

[your name]

2. Approved, with the booking link. When I tag the request.

Hey [first name], it's [your name]! I just went over your custom tattoo request and I would love to get this one done for you.

My current rate, estimated price range, and current availability are all laid out in the private booking page I've linked below 👇🏼

Final step is to grab a date here: [your booking link]

I look forward to working with you, and thanks again for your trust!

3. First check-in. Day 2, if they haven’t booked.

Hope you're doing well! Did you get a chance to check out the info I sent you the other day?

If you have any questions, don't hesitate to ask!

4. Last check-in. Around day 7.

Hey [first name], just checking in one last time before I archive your request! Did you end up finding a date that works for you?

Here's the link one more time in case you need it: [your booking link]

If you have any questions or need help booking feel free to text me here anytime, and if now isn't a good time you can just resubmit your request whenever you're ready 👍🏻

[your name]

5. Booked. The moment the deposit is paid.

Thank you [first name], your tattoo session is booked!

You can find important information about your appointment here: [your prep page]

A reminder text with my suite address will be sent 24 hours before your session.

I'll see you on [date and time]!

6. The day before.

Here's a reminder for your tattoo session tomorrow at [time]!

How to prepare for your session: [your prep page]

Feel free to fill out this consent form before you arrive: [your consent form link]

LOCATION: [studio name], [street address and suite], [city, state, ZIP]

Please take a seat in the lobby and text me when you arrive!

See you tomorrow 👍🏻

7. Aftercare and the review ask. After the session.

[first name], thanks for coming!

Here are your aftercare instructions: [your aftercare page]

You don't have to worry about anything until tomorrow, then just follow the instructions from there... If you have a minute, it'd mean a lot if you left a quick Google review about your session!

[your Google review link]

Thanks again and hope to see you soon!

[your name]

8. Birthday. Every year.

Happy birthday from your tattoo artist, [first name]! 🎂

I think it's time for a birthday tattoo... so here's my gift to you: $50 off your next one, any size, when you book in the next 30 days.

Reply BIRTHDAY and I'll get you set up.

Have a great one!

[your name]

My deposit policy. On my booking page and in every booking.

Deposits are non-refundable but they hold your spot, and I'll work with you to reschedule.
