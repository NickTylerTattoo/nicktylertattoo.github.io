I’m a self-taught artist tattooing out of a private studio in Selden, one client at a time and by appointment only. I picked up a machine in 2022 and taught myself everything the hard way, and since then the work has earned over a hundred five star reviews on Google and a Top 5 spot among Long Island’s favorite tattoo artists. I care about the trust behind those numbers more than the numbers themselves: when someone hands you their skin, you’d better have done your homework.

Custom work is where I’m happiest. I draw every design myself, and the bigger the idea, the more I want to hear about it. Sleeves, back pieces, the large concept you’ve been carrying around for years: booking one here is simpler than you’d expect, one form and one honest conversation and it’s on the calendar. If you can describe it, I can draw it.

The rest of what I do comes back to making this craft matter beyond the appointment book. Once a month I close the books for One Tattoo At A Time, a free night for people marking recovery, remembrance and resilience. And starting in 2027 I’m opening the studio to private events, so your celebration can have a real artist and real tattoos in the room.
