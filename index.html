The artist

Hi, I’m Nick.

I tattoo out of a private studio in Suffolk County, on Long Island. One client at a time, by appointment only. That is not a scheduling quirk. It means nobody is waiting on me while I finish someone else, and your session moves at the pace the piece actually needs.

My work is fine line and ornamental. Florals, geometry, blackwork, the kind of detail that rewards a second look. I have been tattooing under my own name since 2022, and I still draw every piece myself. There is no charge for design or preparation, because the drawing is the part I care most about getting right.

Once a month I close the books and run One Tattoo At A Time, a free event for people marking recovery, remembrance and resilience. The tattoos are numbers and dates. Clean time, a milestone, the day everything changed, a person somebody refuses to forget. It costs nothing and it always will. Artists from New Jersey to Ontario to Poland have since opened their chairs to the same promise.

If you already know what you want, the collection is drawn and ready. If you are carrying something that does not exist yet, tell me about it.
