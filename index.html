I tattoo out of a private studio in Suffolk County, one client at a time and by appointment only. My work is fine line and ornamental: florals, geometry, blackwork, the kind of detail that rewards a second look.

I draw every piece myself, because the drawing is the part I care most about getting right.

Once a month I close the books for One Tattoo At A Time, a free night for people marking recovery, remembrance and resilience.
