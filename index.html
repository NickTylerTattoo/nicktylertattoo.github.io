The artist

Based on Long Island, New York, Nick Tyler specializes in fine line, ornamental, geometric and detailed illustrative tattooing. Every piece — from a palm-sized pre-drawn design to a full back — is conceived with intention and executed with precision.

Work is by appointment only, in a private suite in Suffolk County. There is no charge for design or preparation on custom work — a deposit is taken once the concept is locked, and it comes off the session.
