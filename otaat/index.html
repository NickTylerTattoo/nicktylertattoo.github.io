001 — Why this exists

Ink that says: still here.

#OneTattooAtATime is a completely free monthly tattoo event aimed at changing the way the world views addiction, relapse, loss, and recovery.

Every piece is a number or a date — clean time, a milestone, the day everything changed, the person you carry. Ten characters, typewriter style, tattooed in a private studio. One story at a time.

002 — How it works

Simple. Sacred.
First come, first served.

No applications, no gatekeeping — an honor system for people who need it.

  • 01
    The announcement

    At the start of each month the event date is announced on Instagram @nicktylertattoo.

  • 02
    Registration

    A sign-up link is shared the day before the event. Spots are limited — first come, first served, on the honor system.

  • 03
    Arrive & receive

    Come as you are — sober and ready. Only one client in the studio at a time, so your story stays yours.

The rules — read before you register
  • Numbers or dates only
  • Max 10 characters
  • Typewriter-style font
  • Completely free — no tipping required
  • Sober, substance-free event
  • One client in the studio at a time
  • Must connect to addiction, recovery, or loss

Birthdays, anniversaries and angel numbers don't qualify — this ink is reserved for the fight.
