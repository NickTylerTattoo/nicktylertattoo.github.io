001 · Why this exists

Ink that says: still here.

#OneTattooAtATime is a completely free monthly tattoo event aimed at changing the way the world views addiction, relapse, loss, and recovery.

Every piece is a number or a date: clean time, a milestone, the day everything changed, the person you carry. Ten characters, typewriter style, tattooed in a private studio. One story at a time.

002 · How it works

Simple. Sacred.
First come, first served.

No applications, no gatekeeping. An honor system for people who need it.

  • 01
    The announcement

    At the start of each month the event date is announced on Instagram @nicktylertattoo.

  • 02
    Registration

    A sign-up link is shared the day before the event. Spots are limited. First come, first served, on the honor system.

  • 03
    Arrive & receive

    Come as you are, sober and ready. Only one client in the studio at a time, so your story stays yours.

The rules: read before you register
  • Numbers or dates only
  • Max 10 characters
  • Typewriter-style font
  • Completely free, no tipping required
  • Sober, substance-free event
  • One client in the studio at a time
  • Must connect to addiction, recovery, or loss

Birthdays, anniversaries and angel numbers don't qualify. This ink is reserved for the fight.

003 · The Post

One post started everything.

On my clean date this February I told a stranger's story and made a promise under it. Two million views later, the comments have carried it further than I ever could.

2M+
Views · 196K hearts
I'm offering these tattoos for free.
Because I just heard a story that shook me…
@nicktylertattoo2/22/2012 · 5,112 days
Read the full post

A grieving mother asked for one number on her wrist. 392. The days her daughter stayed clean before addiction took her life.

Not her name. Not the date she passed. The days she fought, and the days she lived free.

That story hit home, because addiction isn't abstract to me. I've had my own day 392, my own day 91, my own hour 14, and plenty of day zeros before finding my way here: 5,112 days, counted from 2/22/2012. So I made the promise that became this page. Clean date and sobriety memorial tattoos, completely free. For anyone in recovery. For anyone honoring someone they lost. For any amount of time. 1 day. 100 days. 14 hours. Even 0.
