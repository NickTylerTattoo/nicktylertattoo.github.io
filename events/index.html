001 — What it is

The studio comes to your party.

A private tiny tattoo flash session at your wedding venue, shower, bachelorette party, or the after party.

I bring the full studio to you. Your guests pick small fine line pieces from a flash menu drawn for the occasion, spend 15 to 25 minutes in the chair, and get back to the party with a souvenir they will never lose. One flat rate for the event. No per-tattoo surprises for your guests.
